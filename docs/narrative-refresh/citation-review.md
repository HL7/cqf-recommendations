## Corrections for integration

1. Narrow S16's wording so readers do not infer that all 465 rules were implemented in CQL; only 12 CQL files/rules were developed and tested in the paper, with additional CQL work ongoing.
2. Remove G05's assertion that no infectious/thrombotic complications were identified. The article reports clinically important treatment delays and says the study did not collect enough follow-up to assess their consequences or establish net benefit.
