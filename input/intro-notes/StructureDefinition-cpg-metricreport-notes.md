- `measure` targets CPGComputableMetric. Subject, date, period, improvement notation, and at least one group with at least one population are required.
- The subject and report type are not narrowed to an individual CPGPatient report; use their actual base-resource semantics and selected evaluation mode.
- Evaluated resources and optional case-feature metadata provide context. A calculated indicator is not itself evidence that care was delivered or a goal achieved.
