- A name is required. Status, subject, encounter, period, managing organization, and telecom are prohibited.
- Participant members, when included, reference CPGPractitionerRoleDefinition or another CPGCareTeamDefinition. They do not identify assigned practitioners.
- A local implementation maps these role requirements to an actual patient-specific care team using its own operational arrangements.
