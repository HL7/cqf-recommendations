- Name, mode, and at least one type are required; mode is fixed to `kind`.
- Identifiers, status, contact/address/position, managing organization, parent location, operating hours, availability exceptions, and endpoints are prohibited. These exclusions keep operational-site details out of the definition.
- The required type expresses the setting category; it does not identify which local facility will deliver care.
