- Status, name, and a CPGPatient subject are required. An encounter reference, when present, targets CPGEncounter.
- Participant members are limited to CPGPractitioner, CPGPractitionerRole, CPGOrganization, CPGRelatedPerson, CPGPatient, or another CPGCareTeam; managing organization targets CPGOrganization.
- Participant roles and periods supply context for the team; membership alone does not determine authorization for every proposed activity.
