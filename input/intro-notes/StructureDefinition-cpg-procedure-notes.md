- `instantiatesCanonical` links to the definition, and `basedOn` can identify the request being fulfilled.

- `status` and `statusReason` follow Procedure semantics. `code`, `subject` and `encounter` identify the procedure and context.

- `reasonReference` carries pertinent supporting information. Case-feature definition, asserted/inferred type and pertinence extensions relate the procedure to the guideline’s patient view.
