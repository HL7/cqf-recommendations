- The plan type includes `order-set`. At least one action is inherited, and each action has a required title.
- Grouping, selection, required, precheck, and cardinality behaviors are must-support. Interpret their configured values rather than assuming every displayed item is selected or mandatory.
- This profile does not add the narrower action-definition target restrictions used by strategy or pathway definitions.
