### L2 Checklist

Use this checklist after collecting L1 narrative content and before or during computable formalization. It helps guideline teams and knowledge engineers capture the scope, recommendation context, terminology, actions, and maintenance decisions needed to develop L3 content. Keep each item label, status, and notes together. The checklist status is not a separate declaration of implementation-guide conformance.

Use the [Represent step](methodology-represent.html#represent-step) to develop the reviewable specification.
